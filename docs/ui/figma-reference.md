## Visual DNA To Borrow
- Fixed left sidebar with active-route pill and compact icon+label navigation.
- Large top header region that gives each page a strong identity.
- Three-zone desktop layout: navigation rail, main work area, right context rail.
- Soft off-white app background with white cards and pale blue-violet accents.
- Rounded cards, generous internal padding, and quiet shadows.
- Dashboard metric cards that combine number/status/progress rather than only static counts.
- Right rail for calendar, selected item detail, timeline, or contextual actions.
- Compact card collections with progress bars, metadata rows, and action affordances.
- Mobile adaptation that replaces the left rail with a top menu button and stacked content.

## What Not To Copy Blindly
- The reference is a generic task dashboard, not a client operations platform.
- Decorative skeleton-like placeholder shapes should become real DigiColony data.
- The Help Center card does not map directly to launch scope; use that space for support, onboarding, or operator notes only if product-approved.
- Blue-violet accent can guide the system, but AI audit and client confidentiality rules stay governed by the DPAF decisions.

## Translation For This Product
- The right rail becomes the place for operational memory: today's focus, selected work item, activity, assets, context, or next actions.
- The central content should prioritize work scanning and record continuity.
- The sidebar should remain simple: Board, Clients, and later Governance.
- The page shell should make admin work feel focused and modern without hiding dense operational controls.

## Design Guardrails
- Preserve accessibility, contrast, and keyboard behavior over visual mimicry.
- Keep client-user views free of AI-agent language.
- Do not introduce decorative imagery unless it represents actual client/project/work context.
- Use the Figma reference as layout and tone, not as a literal content model.
